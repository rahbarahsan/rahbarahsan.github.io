Deciding which AI models a public institution can build on

Agents that collect the facts on every open source model

Federal public service · 2024 to now

Before a model from Hugging Face or Ollama is assessed, an agentic layer fills 28 metadata fields for it, including licence, provenance, training data and known vulnerabilities. That metadata is checked against the AI policy. It decides which security checks apply and helps us set the model's risk profile and the mitigation guidance teams follow.

Assessment needs evidence before anyone looks at a model, so the agents gather it first. Agent profiles were A/B tested on a held out set, and only the best one was promoted.

Built withAzure AI Foundry Agents · LangChain Deep Agents

Carleton University · 2021 · First author

The rarest attack class has 52 training records out of almost 126,000. I compared classical models and neural networks across resampling methods, judged by weighted F1 instead of accuracy.

Accuracy hid how badly single models did on the rare attacks, because normal traffic dominates.

Carleton University · 2021 · First author

AEGAN combines adversarial environment reinforcement learning with a conditional GAN that generates synthetic minority samples. Impersonation attacks were labelled correctly 72% of the time, up from 36%.

Impersonation attacks read as normal traffic fell from 64% to 27%.

Hackathons

Checking "Made in Canada" claims with signed supply chains

Red Team DefTech Hackathon · Ottawa · May 2026 · One of three third-place teams of ten, team Maple Stamp

The Verified Canadian Supply Chains challenge. Buy Canadian claims for defence purchases rest on suppliers' word. Our prototype makes every supplier step a signed record, checks the whole chain for forged, tampered or replayed records, and works out the product's Canadian content.

I built the verification backend, which scored 95.7% on the organisers' self-test over the 1,000 training chains. The team framed the problem and built the interface.

Every forgery and tamper type was caught. The statistical checks on cost, labour and timing scored lower.

Built withPython · FastAPI · Ed25519 signatures · React · TypeScript · Docker
