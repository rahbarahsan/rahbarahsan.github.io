Checking "Made in Canada" claims with signed supply chains

Red Team DefTech Hackathon · Ottawa · May 2026 · Third place, team Maple Stamp

The Verified Canadian Supply Chains challenge. Buy Canadian claims for defence purchases rest on suppliers' word. Our prototype makes every supplier step a signed record, checks the whole chain for forged, tampered or replayed records, and works out the product's Canadian content. A purchaser scans a QR code and sees verified or review required.

I built the verification backend, the system behind the 95.7% score on the organisers' 1,000-chain test. The team framed the problem and built the interface.

The cryptography catches forgeries. The hard cases were honest-looking records with numbers that didn't add up.

Built withPython · FastAPI · Ed25519 signatures · React · TypeScript · Docker
